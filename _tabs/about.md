Hi! I’m Uzurly, 16 years old, from Belgium.  
I’m passionate about offensive cybersecurity: CTFs, Active Directory environments, and privilege escalation. I enjoy taking a system apart step by step to understand exactly where the trust boundaries break.

I write detailed, technical writeups to share what I learn from each challenge — not just the winning command, but the reasoning that got me there.
